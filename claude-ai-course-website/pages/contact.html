Common issues — quick fixes

I paid but there is no download link

Open your success page again with your order reference and press Email me a new link, then check spam. If it still does not arrive, email us with your payment ID — we will verify it manually.

My download link has expired

Expected — links are short-lived and single-use so the PDF cannot be passed around. Ask for a fresh one at no cost; make sure you have a verified order.

The download will not start

Tap the Download your course PDF button rather than waiting for an automatic download, and try a different browser (Chrome or Safari). On some phones you may need to allow downloads for the site.

Money was deducted but the payment failed

Failed authorisations are usually released by your bank automatically. If it does not return within a few working days, email us your payment ID and we will follow it up with Razorpay.

See the refund policy — it lists exactly when we refund and what to include in your email.
