1. Why digital goods are treated differently

The Product is a digital PDF delivered immediately and permanently to your device. Once it has been downloaded, we cannot take the file back, so — as with most digital downloads — a change of mind after downloading is not, by itself, a refundable reason.

2. When we will refund in full

We refund the full amount of ₹1,449 in these situations:

  • You were charged but never received access. A payment succeeded, but our system failed to deliver your link after you gave us a working email address, and we could not deliver it within a reasonable time.
  • Duplicate payment. You were charged twice for the same purchase — the extra charge is refunded in full.
  • Wrong amount charged. The amount debited does not match the price shown at checkout.
  • You received the wrong product. The file delivered is not the Product described on this site.
  • You bought it twice by mistake, and speak to us before downloading the second copy.
  • The file is materially broken — for example, it cannot be opened at all or pages are unreadable — and we cannot supply a working copy.

3. When we will not refund

  • Change of mind after the PDF has been downloaded.
  • The content is not what you expected, where the description on this site already set out what the Product is (a written PDF, not video lessons, with no software included).
  • You expected a different result, such as income, views or business growth. We make no such promises; see section 6 of the Terms.
  • You no longer need it, or you bought it for someone else who changed their mind.
  • You breached the licence — for example, by sharing or re-uploading the file.
  • A payment failure where no money was actually taken. Failed attempts are not charges, and are usually released automatically by the bank.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Email support@example.com within 7 days of the purchase.
  2. Include your order reference and Razorpay payment ID (both are shown on your success page and in your email), and the email address you paid with.
  3. Tell us briefly what went wrong. If it is a technical problem, we may first try to fix it — a working download is usually faster than a refund.

5. What happens next

  • We acknowledge every request, normally within 2 working days.
  • We aim to reach a decision within 7 working days of receiving the details we need.
  • Approved refunds are made through Razorpay, to the original payment method only. We cannot send a refund to a different account.
  • After we approve it, the money normally reaches you within 5 to 10 working days, depending entirely on your bank or card issuer. That timing is outside our control.
  • If we approve a refund after access was already given, your licence to use the Product ends, and you agree to delete any copy you hold.

6. Charged but no access? Do this first

Most "charged but no download" cases are solved in minutes, so please try this before requesting a refund:

  1. Open your success page again with your order reference, and use the “Email me a new link” button.
  2. Check the spam, promotions and updates folders for the email.
  3. Still stuck? Email support with your payment ID — we will verify your payment manually and deliver the PDF. Please do not pay a second time.

7. Consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under applicable consumer protection law, including the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (India) where it applies. If you believe your statutory rights override a decision made under this policy, tell us and we will review the case on that basis.
