Sample prompt cards

Three prompts from the library inside the course. Copy them and try them today.

Plan the edit

Planning 
You are helping me plan a short video edit.

Topic: [topic]
Audience: [who will watch it]
Length: [target duration]
Tone: [calm / energetic / instructional]

Give me:
1. A one-sentence objective for the video
2. A 4-part structure with an approximate duration for each part
3. A shot list for each part
4. Three things I should cut before I start

Tighten a script

Script 
Here is a rough script for my video:

[PASTE SCRIPT]

Rewrite it so that:
- Every sentence is under 20 words
- The first 5 seconds state the value clearly
- No filler phrases, no repetition
- Any jargon is replaced with plain words

Then list every change you made and why, in a short table.

Final review pass

Quality check 
Act as a video quality reviewer before export.

Video: [topic and length]
Format: [platform and aspect ratio]

Check and report on:
1. First 5 seconds — is the hook clear?
2. Pacing — where would a viewer drop off, and why?
3. Audio — any sections likely to be too loud or too quiet?
4. Captions and on-screen text — anything unclear or too fast to read?
5. Export — what settings should I use for this platform?

Answer as a checklist. Only flag real problems.

Prompts are plain text — copy them into your own AI assistant. Replace anything inside square brackets with your own details.

Chapter 3 — Prompt fundamentals

Context. Constraints. Output shape.

Every useful prompt carries three things: what the AI needs to know, what it must not do, and what the answer should look like. Most disappointing AI output is missing one of the three — usually the third.

Chapter 7 — When AI gets it wrong

Recover without losing the edit.

If a generated cut plan ignores your constraints, do not re-prompt blindly. Find the missing constraint, add it in one sentence, and ask for the plan again — with a word limit.
